### Completed Individual Forms

Completed forms remain discrete clinical artefacts and should preserve authorship, completion state and provenance.

### Clinical B4SC Summary

Potential content includes completed/outstanding components, findings, outcomes, referrals, recommendations, follow-up and completion status.

Candidate representation is `Composition`, potentially in a `Bundle`, referencing relevant `QuestionnaireResponse`, `Consent`, `Observation` and `ServiceRequest` resources.

### Whānau B4SC Summary

The whānau-facing summary presents relevant results and next steps in an accessible format. It should not expose internal workflow information, detailed scoring constructs or unsuitable clinical information.

> **Provisional:** Detailed summary content and FHIR representation remain subject to design confirmation.
