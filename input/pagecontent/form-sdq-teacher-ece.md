### SDQ Teacher/ECE Form

The SDQ Teacher/ECE Form is a separate, licensed assessment completed by an ECE educator or teacher. It is separate from the parent form because author and completion context differ.

| Attribute | Draft definition |
|---|---|
| Primary author | ECE educator / teacher |
| Pattern | Single-author |
| Licensing | Licensed assessment |
| Candidate FHIR resources | `Questionnaire`, `QuestionnaireResponse`, `Patient`, `Practitioner` or `RelatedPerson` as appropriate, `Organization`, `Observation` |
| Population | Limited to permitted context; author representation remains to be confirmed |

> **In progress:** Author identity, organisation attribution, access pattern, licensing and scoring representation.
