## Versioning approach

The guide version identifies the version of the complete published Implementation Guide. Individual canonical FHIR artefacts may also carry their own versions where required.

Before formal publication, the programme should confirm:

- the semantic-versioning policy;
- the relationship between guide, API, form, profile, and terminology versions;
- compatibility and migration expectations;
- how corrected or withdrawn versions are published; and
- whether a package-list is required by the selected publication process.
