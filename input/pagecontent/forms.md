# Forms and Questionnaires

One catalogue for B4SC forms. A listed form is **not** a published FHIR Questionnaire unless its generated artifact exists. Clinical content and licences require approval.

| Form | Completed by | Review / completion rule | FHIR definition | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| <a id="consent"></a>Consent | Whānau | Nurse review required | [Draft Questionnaire](Questionnaire-B4SCConsent.html) | In development |
| <a id="whanau-health"></a>Whānau Health | Parent / caregiver | Nurse review where required | [Draft Questionnaire](Questionnaire-B4SCHealthNursing.html) | In development |
| <a id="peds-r"></a>PEDS-R | Parent and nurse | Parent contribution and nurse component | [Draft Questionnaire](Questionnaire-B4SCPEDSR.html) | Licensed content: confirm |
| <a id="sdq-parent"></a>SDQ Parent | Parent and nurse | Parent contribution and nurse component | [Draft Questionnaire](Questionnaire-B4SCSDQParent.html) | Licensed content: confirm |
| <a id="sdq-teacherece"></a>SDQ Teacher / ECE | Educator, where required | Review and workflow to confirm | [Draft Questionnaire](Questionnaire-B4SCSDQTeacher.html) | Proposed |
| <a id="vision-and-hearing"></a>Vision and Hearing | Screening provider | Screening completion evidence | [Draft Questionnaire](Questionnaire-B4SCVisionHearing.html) | In development |
| <a id="nurse-evaluation"></a>Nurse Evaluation | Nurse | Final clinical completion gate | [Health/Nursing draft](Questionnaire-B4SCHealthNursing.html) | Separate Nurse Evaluation Questionnaire not yet defined |

Forms, consent and Vision and Hearing may progress in any order or concurrently. See [B4SC workflow](b4sc-workflow.html) for Task completion rules and [FHIR Artifacts](artifacts.html) for published Questionnaire definitions.


## Questionnaire source files

The six draft FSH definitions are in `input/fsh/questionnaires/` and should be generated by SUSHI into FHIR JSON. Matching **reference JSON drafts** are provided in `reference/questionnaire-json-drafts/` for review only. Do not place those JSON copies into `input/resources/` while the FSH definitions are present, or duplicate resources may result.

**Not yet complete:** These are draft/questionnaire scaffolds. Confirm original form wording, answer options, branching, validation, clinical approvals and licensing before use.
